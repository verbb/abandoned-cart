<h1 align="center">Abandoned Cart for Craft CMS</h1>

Abandoned Cart is a Craft CMS plugin that recovers Craft Commerce sales by emailing customers about carts they left behind and bringing them straight back to checkout.

A ready-made sequence of three reminders catches every abandoned cart, so a store can start recovering sales as soon as it's switched on. Add more sequences when some carts deserve different emails, such as a more personal series for high-value carts or a discount for first-time customers. The Overview shows what recovery earns, which emails bring customers back, and why other carts didn't return.

## Features

- Send sequences of up to five emails, timed from when a cart goes quiet, that stop as soon as the customer orders or unsubscribes.
- Target sequences by cart value, returning or first-time customers, products, any Commerce order condition, or site, with a default sequence catching the rest.
- Start new sequences from presets, and check which sequence a real cart would get before turning it on.
- Write each email's subject, heading, message and button in the control panel, with personalisation tokens, a live preview, and test sends.
- Use the responsive bundled email design with no template files, or your own Twig templates.
- Include a Commerce coupon code that's applied to the cart when the customer returns.
- Restore carts from secure recovery links, with sign-in required for carts that belong to an account.
- Give customers one-click unsubscribing from their inbox, and let signed-in customers manage cart reminders from their account.
- Review every abandoned cart's journey, see why skipped carts weren't emailed, and send, stop or retry emails by hand.
- Look up customers, their carts and recovered revenue, and add anyone to Do Not Email.
- Respect consent with an opt-in field, blocked domains, a returning-customers-only option and a contact limit for frequent abandoners.
- Measure recovered revenue, recovery rate and revenue per email, with a recovery funnel and per-email results.
- Check the scheduler, sending and templates from System Status, and pause all sending in an emergency.
- Give staff access with separate permissions for sequences, Do Not Email and System Status.
- Extend matching, consent, links, rendering and sending with developer events.
